How to scan documents correctly

Passport photo page

Full page, flat, no glare. All 4 corners visible. Text must be readable.

Certificates

Questions

Document FAQ

Common questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often about documents and processing.

My passport is about to expire. Can I still apply?
No — most countries require at least 6 months of validity from your travel date. Renew your passport first, then apply.
My name is spelled differently on different documents. What do I do?
This is the most common cause of rejection. Get the name corrected on your passport first, then re-issue or correct other documents to match.
Do I need to attest my certificates?
For most Gulf countries, attestation is required for education and experience certificates. Costs and timelines vary — ask us for the exact list for your destination. [VERIFY]
I lost my passport. What now?
File a police report, then apply for a new passport. You cannot apply for a work visa without a valid passport. We can guide you through the steps.
What if my documents get rejected?
We check every document before submission to catch problems early. If a document is rejected, we tell you exactly what to fix and how.
