Your protection

Know before you apply

Written offer letter

Signed by the employer, stating job title, salary and contract length.

Employer's real name

Verify the company exists. Ask for the licence or registration number.

Salary in the contract

What is promised verbally must match what is written. If it does not, do not sign.

Correct visa type

A work visa, not a visit visa. A visit visa does not give you the right to work.
